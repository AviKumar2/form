Dehradun

Dehradun, often hailed as the “Gateway to the Himalayas,” beckons travelers to a mesmerizing journey in the northern expanse of India. As the capital of Uttarakhand, this city gracefully cradles itself in the foothills of the majestic Himalayan range, offering an enticing blend of natural beauty, cultural diversity, educational prominence, and abundant adventure opportunities.
