Neeraj Ganga Heritage Palace

The Neeraj Ganga Heritage Palace in Rishikesh is a place which combines all sorts of comforts of luxurious accommodation. The property is set close to the sacred Ganga River and offers its guests comfortable accommodations that are nicely furnished and offer spectacular river views. The place is suitable not only for those who travel with their loved ones or alone but also for those who come to find some spirituality in their journey. This hotel is ideally located just minutes away from such popular tourist spots as Ram Jhula, Laxman Jhula and Triveni Ghat.

Neeraj Ganga Heritage Palace features various room options including Deluxe Rooms, Premier Ganga View Rooms, Maharaja Palace Suites, Family Suites with Garden View, and Family Suites with Ganga View Rooms. Additional amenities include jungle safaris, free parking, bonfires, and a restaurant.

Whether planning a pilgrimage trip or a vacation, Neeraj Ganga Heritage Palace is your best choice to stay in Rishikesh. Book your stay now and enjoy a blend of luxury and local charm.
