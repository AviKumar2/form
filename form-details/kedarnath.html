Kedarnath Temple

Nestled in the majestic Himalayas, Kedarnath Temple is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva and one of India's most sacred pilgrimage destinations.

About Kedarnath

Situated at an altitude of 3,583 meters in Uttarakhand, Kedarnath Temple is believed to have been built by the Pandavas and later revived by Adi Shankaracharya. Every year, millions of devotees undertake this spiritual journey amidst breathtaking Himalayan landscapes.

🏔 Altitude

Located at 3,583 meters above sea level, surrounded by snow-covered Himalayan peaks.

🛕 Jyotirlinga

One of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas dedicated to Lord Shiva.

🙏 Pilgrimage

Part of the famous Char Dham Yatra attracting millions of pilgrims annually.

Pilgrimage Journey

The sacred journey to Kedarnath is filled with devotion, scenic beauty, and unforgettable experiences.

1. Reach Sonprayag

Begin your spiritual journey from Sonprayag, the gateway to Kedarnath.

2. Gaurikund

Start the famous 16 km trek from Gaurikund through the Himalayas.

3. Kedarnath Temple

Offer prayers to Lord Shiva and witness the breathtaking Himalayan surroundings.
