Chopta

Chopta is a beautiful hill station in the Rudraprayag district of Uttarakhand, located at an altitude of around 2,600–2,700 meters (8,500–8,900 ft) above sea level. Surrounded by dense forests, alpine meadows, and snow-capped Himalayan peaks, it is one of the most peaceful destinations in the Garhwal Himalayas.
